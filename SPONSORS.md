# Sponsors

WGO is free, MIT licensed and built in my spare time. If it saves you from paying for a hosted analytics or
observability service, or you just like keeping your data in your own AWS account, please consider sponsoring
its development.

**[Sponsor WGO on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/ljacobsson)**

## What sponsorship pays for

- New features: more of the analytics, tracing and alerting that hosted services charge for.
- Fixes and support: time to triage issues and answer questions.
- Keeping up with AWS: new runtimes, deprecations and service changes, so a deploy keeps working.
- Testing: the AWS accounts used to deploy and test WGO end to end.
